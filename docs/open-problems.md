# Open problems

Ordered by how much they matter.

1. **Nobody has read this cold.** Every rule so far has been judged by its designers. Test B is an empirical claim. Procedure: hand someone the fish story (`examples/fish-and-bird.sem`) with no legend and record where they stall; run a second reader on the same text with 🔹 word breaks restored. That settles the no-break decision and the transparency ratings in one afternoon.
2. **The patient slot is a junk drawer.** In the abstract it holds the thing acted on, the standard of comparison, the comparison class and the domain. Readable, but it is where ambiguity pools. If role markers are ever added, this is the evidence for them. Do not add them before a sentence actually misreads.
3. **👉 is overworked.** It points at a thing or at the whole previous sentence, about ten times in the abstract. A human resolves it; a parser cannot, and neither will a tired reader.
4. **∧ and ∨ are off the emoji keyboard.** *and* and *or* are core grammar and the hardest words to type. ➕ is out (arithmetic plus). No emoji candidate found that is a shape, pairs visually, and does not already mean something.
5. **Missing roots with evidence:** *task, result, step, sequence, cover, connector, warning*. From the abstract and the STE fragment. See [ste100.md](ste100.md).
6. **Possessives blur under no-break.** `🏠🙋🌊` reads as *my home is the sea* or *home, I, sea*. Tolerated because the readings do not contradict; it is still the one place "context chooses" was allowed.
7. **Optional hedging.** Mandatory confidence marking was the one feature that made this better than English for science. Dropped for naturalness. A house style for papers could reinstate it per genre without changing the grammar.
8. **Imperative and warnings** have no convention. Proposals in [ste100.md](ste100.md): empty agent slot = command; ⚠️ opener.
9. **Layer 1 ate the zoo.** 🐘🐜🐇🐢🐣🦋 are adjectives now; a children's book has to say 🐾🐇 for rabbit. The cost of spending transparent animals on abstractions.
10. **Glyph ownership.** Unicode and vendors control the alphabet. Any glyph can be redesigned under us. Rule 6 is a mitigation, not a fix.
